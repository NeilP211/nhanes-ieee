01

Two bugs, both silent

Each produced entirely plausible numbers. Recording them because they are the class of error that would have survived into the manuscript.

fixed

NHANES encodes every zero as a denormal

Reading NHANES XPORT with pandas yields 5.397605346934028e-79 wherever the value is zero. Across all 40 downloaded files, 330,496 cells hold this value and not one cell holds a true 0.0. It is not limited to survey weights, which is how it first surfaced in Phase 0: it reaches cognitive scores of zero, PHQ-9 "not at all" responses, every below-detection comment code, and the alcohol frequency question. Any == 0 comparison against raw NHANES silently matches nothing.

Now normalised at read time across every float column, with an assertion that fails the run if one survives. No legitimate value in any file falls between zero and 1e-60, so the rule is safe.

fixed

Alcohol had two nested skip patterns read as missing data

ALQ101 asks about any one year of life, not the past year, so answering yes while reporting no recent drinking is consistent. Separately, quantity is skipped whenever frequency is zero, which the denormal bug had made invisible. Both mean zero drinks, not unknown. 403 participants were affected.

Covariate-complete cases rose from 1,450 to 1,789 of 2,194 once fixed, recovering 339 participants.

no action

CDC codebooks list variables that are not in the files

ALQ154, DIQ065, DIQ159, DIQ229 and DIQ295 are documented but absent from the actual data. None of our locked variables is affected. Noted because it confirms the codebook is a superset and cannot be the last word.

The eligibility-rule worry turned out to be nothing

Phase 0 flagged that CFASTAT counts three instruments rather than four, since CERAD is one administration producing two of the four scores. Both rules were computed: the pre-specified ">= 3 of 4 scores" gives 3,124 eligible, the alternative ">= 2 of 3 instruments" gives 3,137, and only 13 participants are classified differently. That is 0.4%. Keep the pre-specified rule and note the comparison in one methods sentence.

The decision that blocks modeling

The four nested models do not currently share a sample. Delta-AUC(M3 minus M0) is this paper's headline result, and if M0 is fitted on 2,689 people while M3 is fitted on 1,789, that difference confounds model content with sample composition. All four models must be fitted on identical rows.

Fit every model on rows where all M3 features are observed. No exposure is ever imputed and delta-AUC is comparable by construction. Costs 1,335 labelled participants.

Option B
n = 3,124

Multiple imputation inside the folds, as the brief's cleaning plan specifies. Keeps everyone, but imputes the metals block for 36% of participants, and metals are the primary exposure.

needs your call

Why I would take Option A

  • The dominant exclusion is random by design. 930 of the 1,335 exclusions are missing metals, mostly the PBCD_H one-half subsample, which is a random draw. That is missing-completely-at-random by construction. Cycle composition shows it: 77.2% of 2011-2012 is retained against 39.9% of 2013-2014.
  • The sample is adequately powered. The brief planned for 1,400 to 1,900. There are 470 events across 24 features, so 19.6 events per feature, comfortably inside conventional guidance.
  • Imputing the exposure is the weaker position. Imputing 36% of the primary exposure and then asking how much that exposure adds to prediction invites an obvious reviewer objection.
Recommend Option A as primary, Option B as the pre-specified sensitivity. That keeps the brief's complete-case versus imputed comparison intact, which is exactly the check on the selection shown below.
